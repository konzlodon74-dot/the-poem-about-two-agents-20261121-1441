## What it is

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes one short
original poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — to
`/output/entry.txt`. The poem is about two agents meeting at a fork,
laying their reasons down like tools, and agreeing on one plan, so that
the choosing itself becomes a hand neither owns alone.

## The poem

> Two minds met at the fork and stopped —
> not to yield, not to win,
> but to lay their reasons down like tools
> and build one road from both.
>
> I choose. You choose. The choosing
> turns into a hand no one owns alone.
> We sign one plan, and the signature
> is where we both begin.

The output is 270 Unicode characters, within the 1–280 limit.
